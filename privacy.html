Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Custom Made Canvas Canopies builds canvas canopies, cattle rails, hunting rails, roof racks, canvas covers, awnings and shadenets from 5 Jan Booysen Street, Annlin, Pretoria, 0182. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Custom Made Canvas Canopies. We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us when you contact us, ask for a quote or have work done. This can include:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • details and photos of your vehicle, trailer, boat, caravan or property;
  • what you would like built or repaired, and any messages you send us;
  • invoicing and payment details needed to complete a job.

The quote form on this website

The quote form does not store or send anything to a server. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • to answer your enquiry and prepare a quote;
  • to plan, build, fit and deliver your order;
  • to invoice you and keep the records the law requires;
  • to follow up on your job or a warranty question.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Photos of our work

We sometimes share photos of finished builds on this website and our Facebook page. We take care not to show number plates, faces or addresses where they can identify you. If you would rather your vehicle or property is not shown, tell us and we will remove it.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure. The map in the contact section is loaded from Google Maps, and links to WhatsApp and Facebook take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it to us, or as long as tax and business law requires. After that we delete or destroy it safely.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and only people who need it to do your job can see it.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. To do this, contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
