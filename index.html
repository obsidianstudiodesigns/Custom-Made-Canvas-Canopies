Good to know

Which vehicles do you build for?

Any bakkie, plus trailers, trucks, boats and caravans. Every build is measured for your specific vehicle, so it does not matter whether you drive a Hilux, Land Cruiser, Ranger, D-Max or Navara.

Steel or aluminium?

Both. Aluminium is lighter and will not rust, steel is the budget-friendly workhorse. Neels will help you choose based on how you use the vehicle.

What is the built-in long-range tank?

A tank built into the aluminium cattle rail, hunting rail or roof rack. It holds 40 to 60 litres of water, diesel, petrol, chemicals or a drink of your choice, and can feed a camp shower.

Do you repair existing canvas, awnings and blinds?

Yes. We make new canvas covers, awnings and roll-up blinds, and repair existing ones.
